Quick add

Add events by typing

Write Gym every Mon and Thu at 7pm or Lunch with Sam tomorrow at 1pm at Cafe Roma. The words it understood light up as you type, and a line shows exactly what will be saved before you press Enter.

Leave out the time and it finds your first free hour. It understands English and Spanish, and it all runs on your phone.

Every screen

Uses the whole screen

One app that changes shape with the space you give it.

Tablets

The full week with a Today panel beside it, and an event's details next to the grid when you pick one.

Phones

A three-day week, day and month views, and an agenda, with quick add always at hand.

Keyboard and mouse

Shortcuts for every action, right-click menus, Ctrl+scroll to zoom the hours and Ctrl+P to print the week.

Foldables

Made for foldables

Nothing is drawn across the crease, and folding or unfolding keeps your place.

Half open like a book

The week sits on one side of the hinge, and today or the event you picked on the other.

Flat on a table

The grid stays above the fold and the controls sit below it, within reach.

Two windows

Open a second window beside the first and drag events from one to the other.

Your calendars

Your calendars, your phone

Turn on Phone calendars and Quickspan shows the calendars already on your phone, such as Google or Exchange, next to its own. Add, edit, move and delete events in them, and your phone's account syncs the changes as it always has.

Quickspan asks for calendar access only when you turn this on, and works without it.

Privacy

Private by design

No internet permission

The app can't send your events anywhere. You can check it in the permission list on its Play page.

No account, no ads, no analytics

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing tracking you.

Your calendars stay with their accounts

A Google or Exchange calendar keeps syncing through your phone, not through us.

What it doesn't do (yet)

  • No sync of its own between devices. Use a Google or Exchange calendar for that.
  • No time zones: times are your phone's local time.
  • Quick add understands English and Spanish only.
