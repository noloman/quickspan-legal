Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads and no subscription, and everything on this page is free to use. Quickspan Pro is an optional one-time purchase of €4.99 that adds the extras listed under Quickspan Pro.

Does it sync between my devices?

Not by itself. Keep your events in a Google, Outlook or Samsung calendar on your phone (iCloud works through the free DAVx5 app) and turn on Phone calendars in Quickspan: your account syncs them to your other devices.

Which languages does quick add understand?

Typing and voice quick add understand 12 languages: English, Spanish, Dutch, German, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Indonesian and Korean. The app itself is in 23 languages. Voice needs Android 12 or newer with on-device speech recognition. Dates and times follow your phone's settings.

Does it handle time zones?

Yes. Type “3pm London time” or pin an event to a zone in the editor, and Quickspan shows the zone next to its time. Events from your phone's calendars keep their own zone, and the app follows your phone when its zone changes.

Why does it ask for calendar access?

Only to show and edit the calendars already on your phone, and only when you turn that on. Without it, Quickspan works with its own calendars.

Which phones and tablets does it run on?

Android 8 and newer: phones, tablets, foldables and flip phones, including desktop-style windows on tablets.

What doesn't it do yet?

Quick add understands 12 languages today. Tell us what you miss at support@quickspan.app.
